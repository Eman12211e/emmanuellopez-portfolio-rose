About Me

Hello! ♡

I'm Emmanuel Lopez, a mechanical engineer who also does some software engineering. I volunteer in undergraduate lab research, where I work on structural optimization and 3D-printed materials.

For my first research project, I studied density and sensitivity filtering in topology optimization using the 88-line MATLAB code (Andreassen et al., 2011). I then compared that benchmark code against a graduate mentor's research code to find where their filtering approaches and results differ.

Right now I'm designing lattice patterns for research on measuring Poisson's ratio, 3D-printed in flexible TPU.
